Never miss a meeting again.

A few minutes before each meeting, a little airliner flies across your screen towing your reminder. One click joins: Teams and Zoom open straight in their apps, Google Meet, Webex and others in your browser.

Get it from the Microsoft Store

Free for Windows 10 and 11. No account, no ads.

Or in a terminal: winget install 9P13SJFZP6DD -s msstore

Hard to miss. Never in your way.

A plane before every meeting

The meeting's name, when it starts, and a Join now button. It flies in your real sky: golden at sunset, its lights on at night, into a cloud that matches the weather.

Quiet on calls

On a call, presenting or sharing your screen? It comes small and silent in a corner, and people watching your screen don't see it. When it matters, it flies in big.

Fly where you like

Across the screen, small in a corner, or a spot you pick.

Snooze or running late

Snooze, or tell the organizer you're running late, in one click.

Your day at a glance

A widget with your next meeting, the weather and the air quality, plus a boarding pass for the day.

25 festivals

Each with its own animation and sound; Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas, Lunar New Year, Halloween and India's national days fly in their own liveries. In 24 languages.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your calendar is read on your computer and stays there. Works with Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com, Outlook (classic) on your PC, iCloud, Fastmail, Yahoo, Zoho, GMX, WEB.DE, Nextcloud, Synology or any CalDAV calendar, and calendar links (.ics), Google Calendar's private address included. Read the privacy policy.

With Google Calendar, Meetbell can sign in with your Google account to read your events and the list of your calendars (read-only), only to show your reminders on your computer; nothing is sent to us. Until Google sign-in is available, use Google Calendar's private address.
