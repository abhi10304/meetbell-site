Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Meetbell is a desktop app that reminds you of your meetings. It is built so that nothing about you leaves your computer except what is needed to show your calendar and the weather, and none of it reaches us: the app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app reads, and where it goes

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Your calendar events (title, time, meeting link, location, organizer and attendee addresses) To fly a reminder before each meeting, open the meeting, mark VIP meetings and write the "running late" message Read directly from Microsoft 365 / Outlook.com, Google Calendar, a calendar (.ics) link you add, or classic Outlook on this computer, using read-only access. Kept in memory on your computer. Never sent anywhere else.
Sign-in tokens for Microsoft or Google (only if you connect those) To keep reading your calendar without signing in again Stored on your computer, encrypted by Windows (DPAPI) or the macOS Keychain. Sent only to Microsoft or Google. Removed when you sign out.
The city you choose for the weather (its name and map position) To show weather and air quality in the widget The map position (latitude and longitude) is sent to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, from your computer. No account and nothing about you is sent.
Whether an app is using your microphone or camera right now, and whether a full-screen app is running (Windows) To make reminders small and silent while you're on a call, sharing your screen or presenting Checked on your computer only when a reminder is about to fly. Nothing is recorded or sent.
Your settings, frequent-flyer miles and today's meeting log To remember your choices and show your boarding pass and landing report Stored on your computer in the app's folder. Never sent anywhere.

When you press Running late?, the app opens a Teams chat or an e-mail draft addressed to the organizer. Nothing is sent until you press Send in that app yourself.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal information.
  • It does not track you, show ads, or use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It never writes to your calendar.
  • It does not record your screen, microphone or camera.

Your control

  • Disconnect a calendar from the app's Calendars window; its tokens are deleted (and revoked where the provider supports it).
  • Turn the weather off, or remove your city, in Settings › General.
  • Uninstalling the app removes it; your settings folder can be deleted from %APPDATA%\Meetbell (Windows) or ~/Library/Application Support/Meetbell (Mac).

Children

The app is a work tool and is not directed at children.
